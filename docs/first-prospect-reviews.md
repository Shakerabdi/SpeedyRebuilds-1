## Before sending the drafts

The Urban Painting and Outdoor Solutions drafts remain unsent. Confirm the recipient and a valid contact basis before using them. No mailing address has been supplied or invented.

For example, a completed footer can use:

> [Your name], SpeedyRebuilds
> [Valid business mailing address]
> contact@speedyrebuilds.com | 226-507-5802
> To stop further marketing messages from SpeedyRebuilds, reply “unsubscribe.”

Canada's general commercial-email requirements include consent, sender identification and an unsubscribe mechanism. Public email publication has specific conditions and is not blanket permission. See the [CRTC FAQ](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm) and [guidance on implied consent](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm).
