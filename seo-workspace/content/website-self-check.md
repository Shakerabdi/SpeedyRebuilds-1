# A practical website check for Windsor-Essex business owners

A short self-review from SpeedyRebuilds. You can do these checks without buying a tool or sharing access to your accounts.

## 1. Explain the offer

Open the homepage on your phone. Without scrolling, can a new visitor tell what you do, who you help, where you work, and how to take the next step? Ask someone unfamiliar with your business to describe it after reading the opening section. Replace vague slogans with a clear explanation of the service.

## 2. Read it on a phone

Check whether text is comfortable to read without zooming, buttons are easy to tap, and the page fits the screen without sideways scrolling. Open the menu and try each important link. Check the services and contact sections as well as the first screen.

## 3. Follow the contact path

Try the phone and email links. Check whether the inquiry form asks only for information needed to respond. A quote request may need project details; a short website review may only need a name, email, website address, and optional context. Test your own form with your own details and confirm that the request reaches the intended inbox. Avoid submitting repeated tests to someone else's form.

## 4. Check what the page promises

Are services, prices, delivery times and contact details current? Remove expired promotions and unfinished placeholder text. Describe actual work and real experience. Use testimonials and customer images only with permission, and avoid results you cannot substantiate.

## 5. Inspect the search listing basics

Make sure the page title describes the service and business. Give the page a useful description, one clear main heading and understandable section headings. These elements help explain a page; they do not guarantee Google will display a particular snippet or rank it in a particular position.

## 6. Keep useful content accessible

Check that important text and links appear reliably as the page loads. On a website that relies on JavaScript, a developer can check whether the initial HTML also contains the main content. A blank JavaScript-disabled page is a useful diagnostic signal, although it does not by itself prove the page is absent from Google.

## 7. Choose the smallest useful next step

Write down the three most specific issues you found. For each, record the page, what happens, and what a visitor needs instead. A broken contact link or confusing opening sentence may deserve attention before a complete redesign.
